Privacy Policy — WebSurgeon

Effective date: 11 November 2026 · Developer: tuantv · Contact: tuantruongvan3001@gmail.com

Tiếng Việt bên dưới

Summary

WebSurgeon runs entirely on your device. It has no server, no account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

Nothing you view, copy, hide or convert is sent to the developer or to anyone else.

What the extension does on the pages you visit

WebSurgeon's page script runs on websites you open so that it can do five things — all inside your browser:

FeatureWhat it touchesWhen
Hide elementsApplies the hiding rules you created for that website (CSS selectors). When you aim and click, it reads the clicked element's tag, id/class names and up to 40 characters of its text to build a rule and a label.Rules: whenever you open the site. Reading an element: only while you are aiming (Alt+Z).
Unlock pageRestores scrolling, text selection and right-click, and hides full-screen overlays, by changing styles and event handling of the open page.Only when you press Alt+X or the popup button, or on sites where you turned on "Always unlock".
Clean links on copyWhen you copy, it checks whether the copied text is a single web link and, if so, removes tracking parameters from it. Other text is ignored and nothing is stored.Only at the moment you copy. Can be turned off in Settings.
Images & tablesConverts an image you right-click to PNG/JPG, or a table you right-click to Excel/Markdown text.Only when you pick that item in the right-click menu.
Full-page screenshotScrolls the open page section by section, takes a picture of each visible section with Chrome's screenshot function, and joins them into one PNG. Bars that stay fixed on screen are hidden briefly so they don't repeat; the page is put back exactly as it was.Only when you press Full-page shot in the popup, pick it in the right-click menu, or use a shortcut you assigned.

Screenshots are created in memory and saved straight to your Downloads folder; they are not uploaded or kept by

the extension.

To convert an image, the extension downloads that one image from the address the page already uses, exactly as

your browser does when it shows it, and converts it in memory. The result goes to your Downloads folder or your

clipboard. Images are not sent anywhere else and are not kept by the extension.

The extension does not store the links you copy, the text of pages, your browsing history, images or tables.

What the extension does NOT do

  • It does not transmit page content, copied text, images, rules, settings or usage data off your device.
  • It does not use cookies of its own, fingerprinting, analytics or advertising SDKs.
  • It does not sell, rent or share data with third parties, and does not use data for creditworthiness or lending.
  • It does not load remote code. All code ships inside the extension package.
  • It does not read passwords, form inputs, other tabs or your browsing history.
  • It does not bypass logins, subscriptions or paywalls, and cannot reveal content a website did not send to your browser.

Everything is processed locally.

Exporting and deleting your data

  • Export / import: Settings › Export rules saves your hiding rules as a .json file on your computer;

Import rules merges such a file back. Files are created and read locally.

  • Delete: click "Show again" or "Restore all" in the popup for the current site, turn off "Always unlock",

or remove the extension — Chrome then deletes all its local data.

Children

The extension is a general productivity tool, not directed at children under 13, and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

Tóm tắt

WebSurgeon chạy hoàn toàn trên máy của bạn: không máy chủ, không tài khoản, không thống kê, không quảng cáo,

không theo dõi. Không có gì bạn xem, copy, ẩn hay chuyển đổi được gửi cho nhà phát triển hay bất kỳ ai.

Tiện ích KHÔNG làm gì

  • Không gửi nội dung trang, chữ đã copy, ảnh, quy tắc, cài đặt hay dữ liệu sử dụng ra khỏi máy bạn.
  • Không dùng cookie riêng, dấu vân tay trình duyệt, thống kê hay SDK quảng cáo.
  • Không bán, cho thuê, chia sẻ dữ liệu với bên thứ ba; không dùng dữ liệu để chấm điểm tín dụng hay cho vay.
  • Không tải mã từ xa. Toàn bộ mã nằm trong gói tiện ích.
  • Không đọc mật khẩu, ô nhập liệu, tab khác hay lịch sử duyệt web.
  • Không vượt đăng nhập, gói trả phí hay tường phí, và không thể làm hiện nội dung mà website không gửi tới trình duyệt của bạn.
